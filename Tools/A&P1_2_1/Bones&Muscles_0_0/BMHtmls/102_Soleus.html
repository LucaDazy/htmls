Preview: Soleus

SVG ID: muscle_soleus

Region / Origin: Proximal tibia and fibula

Landmarks / Insertion: Calcaneous via calcaneal tendon

Action: Plantar flexes foot

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right lower leg. The soleus is a broad, flat, powerful muscle situated deep to the gastrocnemius (whose outline is shown faintly for context). It originates from the soleal line of the tibia and the posterior fibula, merging distally into the thick calcaneal (Achilles) tendon to insert on the heel.
