Preview: Tibialis Posterior

SVG ID: muscle_tibialis_posterior

Region / Origin: Tibia and fibula

Landmarks / Insertion: Tarsals and metatarsals

Action: Adducts, inverts, and plantar flexes foot

Note: Visualized from a deep posterior view of the right lower leg. The tibialis posterior is the deepest muscle of the posterior compartment, situated directly on the interosseous membrane between the tibia and fibula. Its tendon descends behind the medial malleolus (left side in this posterior view) and fans out across the plantar surface of the foot to insert on multiple tarsals and metatarsals.
