Preview: Vastus Intermedius

SVG ID: muscle_vastus_intermedius

Region / Origin: Anterior and lateral surfaces of shaft of femur

Landmarks / Insertion: Patella and tibial tuberosity via patellar ligament

Action: Extends lower leg at knee

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right thigh. The Rectus Femoris has been removed (indicated by the dashed outline) to reveal the Vastus Intermedius, which sits directly on the anterior femoral shaft, nestled between the Vastus Lateralis and Vastus Medialis.
