Preview: Stylohyoid

SVG ID: muscle_stylohyoid

Region / Origin: Styloid process of temporal bone

Landmarks / Insertion: Hyoid

Action: Elevates larynx

Note: Visualized from a lateral view of the right neck. The slender stylohyoid muscle descends from the styloid process to the hyoid bone. Distally, the muscle typically splits into two slips that surround the intermediate tendon of the digastric muscle (represented by the gap) before inserting onto the hyoid. The mandible is ghosted to reveal the deeper structures.
