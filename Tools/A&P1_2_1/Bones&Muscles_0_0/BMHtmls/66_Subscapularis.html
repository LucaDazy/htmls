Preview: Subscapularis

SVG ID: muscle_subscapularis

Region / Origin: Subscapular fossa of scapula

Landmarks / Insertion: Lesser tubercle of humerus

Action: Medial rotation of humerus

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right shoulder girdle. The subscapularis is a large, multipennate muscle that fills the subscapular fossa. Its broad fibers converge into a thick tendon that crosses the anterior shoulder joint to insert onto the lesser tubercle. It is the largest of the four rotator cuff muscles.
