Preview: Internal Intercostals

SVG ID: muscle_internal_intercostals

Region / Origin: Superior border of each rib

Landmarks / Insertion: Inferior border of rib above

Action: Depresses ribs during expiration

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the thorax. The internal intercostals are situated deep to the external intercostals. Their fibers run superomedially (upward and inward, like "hands on the chest"). Unlike the external layer, the muscular fibers of the internal intercostals extend all the way anteriorly to the sternum.
