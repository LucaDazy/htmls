Preview: Supraspinatus

SVG ID: muscle_supraspinatus

Region / Origin: Supraspinous fossa of scapula

Landmarks / Insertion: Greater tubercle of humerus

Action: Abduction of the humerus

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right shoulder. The supraspinatus muscle occupies the supraspinous fossa, situated superior to the spine of the scapula. Its tendon passes laterally beneath the acromion process (through the subacromial space) to insert on the superior facet of the greater tubercle.
