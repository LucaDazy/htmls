Preview: Orbicularis Oris

SVG ID: muscle_orbicularis_oris

Region / Origin: Maxillary bone & mandible

Landmarks / Insertion: Lips

Action: Compresses, purses lips (kissing)

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the lower face. The orbicularis oris forms a complex sphincter muscle around the mouth. Its fibers interlace with other facial muscles at the modiolus (corners of the mouth) and insert into the skin of the lips.
