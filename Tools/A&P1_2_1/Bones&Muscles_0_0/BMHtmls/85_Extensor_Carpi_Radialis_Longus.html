Preview: Extensor Carpi Radialis Longus

SVG ID: muscle_extensor_carpi_radialis_longus

Region / Origin: Lateral supracondylar ridge of humerus

Landmarks / Insertion: Base of 2nd metacarpal

Action: Extends and abducts wrist

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right forearm. The extensor carpi radialis longus originates high on the lateral supracondylar ridge. Its fusiform belly sweeps down the lateral aspect of the posterior forearm, transitioning into a long tendon that passes beneath the extensor retinaculum to insert on the dorsal base of the second metacarpal.
