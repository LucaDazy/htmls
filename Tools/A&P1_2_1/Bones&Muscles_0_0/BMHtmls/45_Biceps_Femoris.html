Preview: Biceps Femoris

SVG ID: muscle_biceps_femoris

Region / Origin: Ischial tuberosity & linea aspera

Landmarks / Insertion: Lateral condyle of tibia & fibular head

Action: Flexes lower leg at knee, extends & laterally rotates thigh

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right thigh. The long head originates medially from the ischial tuberosity, crossing laterally over the posterior femur to join the short head, which originates from the linea aspera. They fuse into a common tendon inserting on the lateral side of the knee.
