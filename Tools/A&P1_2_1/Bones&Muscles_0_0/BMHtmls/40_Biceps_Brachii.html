Preview: Biceps Brachii

SVG ID: muscle_biceps_brachii

Region / Origin: Coracoid process of scapula

Landmarks / Insertion: Radial tuberosity

Action: Flexes forearm at elbow & supinates forearm

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right arm. Features the long head (lateral) and short head (medial), merging into a common belly and inserting distally onto the radius, alongside the bicipital aponeurosis fanning medially.
