# Q: Which of the following best represents the two major components of the integumentary system?
? Think about the surface layer versus the associated structures.
- [ ] Epidermis and dermis
  ^ Incorrect. These are the two layers of the cutaneous membrane, which is only one component of the system.
- [x] Cutaneous membrane and accessory structures
  ^ Correct. The integumentary system is divided into the cutaneous membrane (skin) and accessory structures (hair, nails, glands).
- [ ] Stratum corneum and subcutaneous layer
  ^ Incorrect. The stratum corneum is a specific epidermal layer, and the subcutaneous layer is deep to the integument.
- [ ] Dermis and hypodermis
  ^ Incorrect. The hypodermis (subcutaneous layer) is not technically part of the integumentary system itself.
> The integumentary system has two major parts: the cutaneous membrane (which includes the epidermis and dermis) and the accessory structures (hair follicles, exocrine glands, and nails).

# Q: What is the primary physiological function of melanin in the epidermis?
? Think about why skin darkens (tans) after spending a day at the beach.
- [ ] To absorb heat from the environment and warm the body.
  ^ Incorrect. Thermoregulation is managed by blood flow and sweat glands.
- [ ] To prevent the excessive evaporation of water from the stratum corneum.
  ^ Incorrect. Water resistance is primarily provided by keratin and lipid secretions.
- [x] To protect the DNA of epidermal cells from the damaging effects of ultraviolet (UV) radiation.
  ^ Correct. Melanin acts like a sunshade around the nucleus of keratinocytes.

  ^ Incorrect. The liver and kidneys convert vitamin D3 to calcitriol; UV light synthesizes D3 itself, but melanin actually limits UV absorption.
