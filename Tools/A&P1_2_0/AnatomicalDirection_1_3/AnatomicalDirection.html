💡 Rules & Common Traps (Read First)
MULTI-SELECT REQUIRED: Many vectors have more than one valid term (e.g. Ventral AND Anterior). If multiple terms apply, you must click all of them to pass the card.

The "Anatomical Position" Trap: Remember that in bipeds, Anatomical Position means arms are at the sides with palms facing forward. This rotates the radius and thumb to the outside.

The Biped vs Quadruped Trap: Posture changes everything.
  • In humans, the belly faces forward. So Ventral = Anterior.
  • In dogs, the belly faces the floor. So Ventral = Inferior.
  • In dogs, they move nose-first. So Anterior = Cephalad (Head).
The Appendage Trap: When comparing two points on the same limb, always prioritize Proximal/Distal over Superior/Inferior.
