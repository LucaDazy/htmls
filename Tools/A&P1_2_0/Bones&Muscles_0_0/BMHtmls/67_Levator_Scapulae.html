Preview: Levator Scapulae

SVG ID: muscle_levator_scapulae

Region / Origin: Transverse processes of cervical vertebrae (1-4)

Landmarks / Insertion: Scapula

Action: Elevates scapula

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right neck and upper back. The levator scapulae descends from the transverse processes of the first four cervical vertebrae, twisting slightly as its slips converge, to insert on the superior angle and upper medial border of the scapula.
