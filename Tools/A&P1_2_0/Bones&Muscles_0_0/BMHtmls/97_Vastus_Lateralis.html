Preview: Vastus Lateralis

SVG ID: muscle_vastus_lateralis

Region / Origin: Greater trochanter, intertrochanteric line, and linea aspera of femur

Landmarks / Insertion: Patella and tibial tuberosity via patellar ligament

Action: Extends lower leg at knee

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right thigh. The vastus lateralis is the largest and most laterally situated muscle of the quadriceps femoris group. It arises from the proximal femur and forms the prominent lateral bulk of the thigh before converging into the quadriceps tendon.
