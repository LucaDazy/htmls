Preview: Tibialis Anterior

SVG ID: muscle_tibialis_anterior

Region / Origin: Lateral condyle of tibia

Landmarks / Insertion: Base of 1st metatarsal & medial cuneiform bone

Action: Dorsiflexes & inverts foot

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right lower leg. The tibialis anterior is a thick, fleshy muscle situated on the lateral side of the tibia's prominent anterior crest. Its strong tendon crosses the anterior ankle (passing under the extensor retinacula) and dives medially to insert on the inner aspect of the foot.
