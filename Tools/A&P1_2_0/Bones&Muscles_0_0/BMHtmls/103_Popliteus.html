Preview: Popliteus

SVG ID: muscle_popliteus

Region / Origin: Lateral condyle of femur

Landmarks / Insertion: Posterior surface of proximal tibial shaft

Action: Medial rotation of tibia; flexion at knee

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the right knee. The popliteus is a deep, triangular muscle at the back of the knee. Its strong tendon originates on the lateral aspect of the lateral femoral condyle, passing inferomedially to fan out into a fleshy belly that inserts on the posterior tibia above the soleal line. This muscle famously "unlocks" the fully extended knee.
