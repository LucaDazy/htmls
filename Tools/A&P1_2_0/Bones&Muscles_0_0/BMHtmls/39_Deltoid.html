Preview: Deltoid

SVG ID: muscle_deltoid

Region / Origin: Clavicle & scapula

Landmarks / Insertion: Deltoid tuberosity of humerus

Action: Prime mover of humerus abduction; aids in flexion, extension, and rotation of the humerus

Note: Visualized from an anterolateral view of the right shoulder. The deltoid is a large, multipennate muscle that wraps over the shoulder joint, originating from the clavicle anteriorly, and the acromion and spine of the scapula laterally and posteriorly.
