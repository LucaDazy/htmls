Preview: Extensor Digitorum Longus

SVG ID: muscle_extensor_digitorum_longus

Region / Origin: Lateral condyle of tibia & fibula

Landmarks / Insertion: Superior surface of phalanges 2-5

Action: Extends toes 2-5

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right lower leg. The extensor digitorum longus is a pennate muscle located in the lateral part of the anterior compartment of the leg, lateral to the tibialis anterior. Its tendon passes under the extensor retinacula before diverging into four distinct slips that traverse the dorsum of the foot to insert on the lateral four toes.
