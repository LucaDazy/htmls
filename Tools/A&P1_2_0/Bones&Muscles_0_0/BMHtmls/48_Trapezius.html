Preview: Trapezius

SVG ID: muscle_trapezius

Region / Origin: Occipital bone, ligamentum nuchae, and spinous processes of all thoracic vertebrae

Landmarks / Insertion: Clavicle & acromion and spine of scapula

Action: Elevates, retracts, depresses, or rotates scapula; elevates clavicle; extends neck

Note: Visualized from a posterior view of the upper back and neck. This massive, superficial diamond-shaped muscle features a prominent central aponeurosis and distinct upper (descending), middle (transverse), and lower (ascending) fibers.
