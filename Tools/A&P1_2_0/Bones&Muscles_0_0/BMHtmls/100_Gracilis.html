Preview: Gracilis

SVG ID: muscle_gracilis

Region / Origin: Inferior ramus of pubis

Landmarks / Insertion: Tibia (medial surface of proximal shaft)

Action: Flexes lower, and medially rotates leg at knee, adducts thigh at hip

Note: Visualized from an anterior view of the right thigh. The gracilis is the most superficial muscle on the medial side of the thigh. It is a long, strap-like muscle that descends almost vertically from the pubic arch to insert into the pes anserinus on the medial tibia, wrapping slightly posteriorly around the medial condyles.
