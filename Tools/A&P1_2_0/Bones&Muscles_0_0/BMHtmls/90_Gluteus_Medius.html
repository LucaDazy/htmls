Preview: Gluteus Medius

SVG ID: muscle_gluteus_medius

Region / Origin: Lateral surface of ilium

Landmarks / Insertion: Greater trochanter of femur

Action: Abducts and medially rotates thigh

Note: Visualized from a lateral view of the right hip and pelvis. The gluteus medius is a broad, thick, radiating muscle situated on the outer surface of the pelvis. Its posterior third is typically covered by the gluteus maximus (shown faintly for context). Its strong tendon inserts onto the lateral surface of the greater trochanter.
