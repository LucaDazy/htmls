Preview: Mylohyoid

SVG ID: muscle_mylohyoid

Region / Origin: Medial surface of mandible

Landmarks / Insertion: Hyoid

Action: Elevates floor of mouth and hyoid bone; depresses mandible

Note: Visualized from an inferior/submental view (looking up at the jaw from below). The paired mylohyoid muscles stretch from the inner surface of the mandible to a central median raphe and the hyoid bone, forming a muscular "sling" that acts as the floor of the mouth.
