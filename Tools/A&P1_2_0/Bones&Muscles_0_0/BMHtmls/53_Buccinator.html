Preview: Buccinator

SVG ID: muscle_buccinator

Region / Origin: Molar regions of maxilla & mandible

Landmarks / Insertion: Orbicularis oris

Action: Compresses cheeks (whistling)

Note: Visualized from a lateral view of the right face. The buccinator forms the muscular base of the cheek, originating posteriorly from the alveolar processes of the maxilla and mandible, and running horizontally to insert anteriorly into the fibers of the orbicularis oris. Faint silhouettes of the masseter and orbicularis oris are provided for context.
